1. Overview

Plain-English Summary: EntryGlobe PinApp connects to Pinterest on your behalf to help you discover, save, and shop products. We collect only what we need, we never sell your data, and you can revoke access anytime from your Pinterest settings.

This Privacy Policy describes how EntryGlobe PinApp ("EntryGlobe", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you visit our website and use our services (collectively, the "Service"). This Policy applies to all users globally and is designed to comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and Pinterest's API Terms of Service and Developer Guidelines.

By using the Service you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the Service.

3. Pinterest API Usage

EntryGlobe PinApp uses the Pinterest API to provide core features. By connecting your Pinterest account, you authorise us to access Pinterest data on your behalf through OAuth 2.0, as permitted by Pinterest's developer platform. Our use is governed by the Pinterest API Terms of Service, the Pinterest Developer Guidelines, and Pinterest's own Privacy Policy.

3.1 OAuth Scopes We Request

We request only the minimum scopes necessary for the features you use:

ScopePurposeRequired?
boards:readRead your existing Pinterest boards to display and organise contentYes
boards:writeCreate and update boards on your behalf when you chooseOptional
pins:readRead your saved pins to power AI recommendationsYes
pins:writeSave new pins to your boards when you click "Save"Optional
user_accounts:readRead your Pinterest profile name and photo for display in the appYes

We do not request access to your Pinterest password, payment information, or any scope beyond those listed above.

3.2 How We Use Pinterest Data

  • To display your boards and pins within the EntryGlobe interface
  • To enable saving pins to your Pinterest boards through the Service
  • To power AI-based product recommendations personalised to your taste
  • To provide analytics on your board performance (read-only)
  • To synchronise price-alert preferences with your saved pins

3.3 What We Do NOT Do With Pinterest Data

  • We do not sell, rent, or licence Pinterest user data to third parties
  • We do not use Pinterest data for advertising targeting beyond the Service's scope
  • We do not share Pinterest user data with data brokers
  • We do not combine Pinterest API data with other sources to create advertising profiles
  • We do not store Pinterest images on our servers — images are served from Pinterest's own CDN
  • We do not store your Pinterest password — authentication is entirely via Pinterest's OAuth server

7. Pinterest Developer Data Rules (Compliance)

This section describes our compliance with Pinterest's specific requirements for applications built on the Pinterest API, as required by the Pinterest Developer Guidelines.

7.1 Permitted Use of Pinterest API Data

  • We display Pinterest content only to the authenticated user who owns that content
  • We use Pinterest data solely to operate EntryGlobe PinApp's stated features
  • We do not use Pinterest API data to replicate or compete with Pinterest's core product
  • We do not cache Pinterest images on our servers

7.2 Data Minimisation

We access only the Pinterest data fields necessary for each specific feature. Server-side filtering ensures no excess Pinterest data is persisted.

7.3 Token Security

  • Pinterest OAuth access tokens are stored encrypted at rest using AES-256
  • Tokens are transmitted only over TLS-encrypted connections (HTTPS)
  • We never expose user tokens to client-side JavaScript or third-party scripts
  • Tokens are automatically revoked and deleted when a user disconnects their Pinterest account

7.4 Revoking Pinterest Access

You can revoke our access at any time:

  1. Go to Pinterest Settings → Security → Apps & websites
  2. Find "EntryGlobe PinApp" and click Remove access

Or email entryglobe@gmail.com and we will revoke tokens within 48 hours. All Pinterest API data associated with your account will be deleted within 30 days of revocation.

7.5 Third-Party Data Restrictions

We do not allow third parties to access Pinterest user data retrieved via the API, except service providers acting directly on our behalf under binding contractual obligations at least as protective as this Policy.

9. Security
