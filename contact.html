FAQs

Common Questions

Is EntryGlobe PinApp free?

Yes — our core features are completely free forever. We offer optional premium plans with advanced analytics and unlimited boards.

Do you store my Pinterest password?

Never. We use Pinterest's official OAuth 2.0 flow — your password goes only to Pinterest, not to us. We receive a secure access token only.

Can I use this without a Pinterest account?

Yes — you can browse our product catalogue without connecting Pinterest. Connecting Pinterest unlocks saving, board management, and AI recommendations.

How do I delete my data?

Email us at entryglobe@gmail.com with "Delete My Data" in the subject line. We'll process your request within 30 days per our Privacy Policy.

Are you affiliated with Pinterest?

No. We are an independent developer app that uses the official Pinterest API. We are not owned by or affiliated with Pinterest, Inc.

Which countries do you support?

EntryGlobe PinApp is available globally. We comply with GDPR (EU/UK) and CCPA (California) privacy regulations.
